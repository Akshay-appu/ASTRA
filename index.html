FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Astra Digital Market.

What digital marketing services does Astra Digital Market offer? +
We offer Social Media Management, Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO Optimization, Website Design & Development, Branding, Graphic Design, Content Creation, Video Editing, Reels, Lead Generation, WhatsApp Marketing, and Google Business Profile Optimization — all customized to your business goals.
How much does digital marketing cost for a small business? +
Our Starter Package begins at just ₹3,999/month and includes social media posts, Google Business Profile setup, basic SEO, and monthly reporting. We offer flexible packages designed for every budget. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your specific needs.
How long does it take to see results from digital marketing? +
Paid ads (Google/Meta) typically show results within 7–14 days. SEO takes 2–4 months to build momentum. Social media growth is usually visible within 30–60 days. We track all KPIs and share clear monthly reports so you always know what's working.
Do you work with businesses outside Bangarapet? +
Yes! While we are based in Bangarapet, Karnataka, we work with businesses across India — Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and beyond. All our services are delivered remotely with regular calls, reports, and dedicated support.
Will I have a dedicated account manager? +
Absolutely. Every client at Astra Digital Market gets a dedicated account manager who is your single point of contact. They handle strategy, execution, reporting, and are available for questions via WhatsApp and email throughout the month.
Can I see a portfolio or case studies of your work? +
Yes! We have successfully completed 50+ projects across industries like fashion, healthcare, real estate, restaurants, gyms, education, and e-commerce. Contact us directly and we'll share relevant case studies and results from businesses similar to yours.
Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime? +
We offer flexible monthly plans with no long-term lock-in. We recommend a minimum of 3 months to see meaningful results, but you are free to adjust or cancel with 15 days' notice. We believe in earning your trust through results, not contracts.
