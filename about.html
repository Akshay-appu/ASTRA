OUR STORY

Built to bridge the gap between Technology & Growth.

Founded in 2020, Astra Digital Market was established with a singular vision: to give businesses a single, cohesive partner for both modern software engineering and data-driven digital marketing.

Too often, companies hire one agency to build their website or app, and a separate agency to market it — resulting in disconnected strategy, slow execution, and wasted budget. At Astra, we unite custom web and mobile software development with high-performance digital marketing, AI workflow automation, and brand identity design under one roof.
